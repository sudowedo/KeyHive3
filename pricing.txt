KeyHive Pricing Plan (Pre-Launch Draft)
======================================

This pricing model is designed for the current app and explicitly includes the upcoming
"Projects" feature before public launch.

North Star:
- Let users start in <5 minutes.
- Charge based on governance depth + scale (projects, requests, subkeys, logs, controls).
- Keep provider costs pass-through (OpenAI/Anthropic/Google usage billed to user's own keys).


1) Free
-------
Price:
- $0 / month

Who it is for:
- Solo developers evaluating the gateway quickly.
- Early internal demos.

Includes:
- Projects: 1
- Master Keys: 1 total (choose 1 provider from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google)
- Subkeys: 3
- Monthly requests: 10,000
- Rate limiting: Basic
- Logs: Basic recent logs only (no advanced analytics/history)
- Key governance: Limited (pause/revoke + basic limits)
- Team members: 1 admin
- Support: Community / docs only

Purpose:
- "Try in 5 minutes"


2) Starter
----------
Price:
- $19 / month

Who it is for:
- Small teams running one product in production-light mode.

Includes:
- Projects: up to 3
- Master Keys: up to 3 (OpenAI + Anthropic + Google)
- Subkeys: up to 25
- Monthly requests: 250,000
- Rate limiting: Standard per-subkey controls
- Logs: 14-day retention + basic filters
- Key governance: Full policy edits (quota, max requests, expiry, status)
- Alerts: Near-limit + expiry alerts
- Team members: up to 3 admins
- Support: Email (best effort)


3) Growth
---------
Price:
- $99 / month

Who it is for:
- Multi-client startups, agencies, and B2B tools with active traffic.

Includes:
- Projects: up to 15
- Master Keys: up to 15
- Subkeys: up to 250
- Monthly requests: 2,000,000
- Rate limiting: Advanced (tighter policy controls + burst handling)
- Logs: 90-day retention + richer analytics
- Key governance: Advanced (bulk edits, stronger guardrails, audit-focused controls)
- Alerts: Threshold notifications + quota extension workflows
- Team members: up to 10 admins
- Support: Priority email


4) Business
-----------
Price:
- $299 / month (base)

Who it is for:
- High-volume teams needing governance, compliance posture, and operational controls.

Includes:
- Projects: Unlimited
- Master Keys: Unlimited
- Subkeys: Unlimited (fair-use policy)
- Monthly requests: 10,000,000 included
- Overage: usage-based request overage pricing (defined in billing policy)
- Rate limiting: Enterprise-grade policy controls
- Logs: 1-year retention + export-ready access
- Key governance: Enterprise policy suite + stricter operational controls
- Team members: 25 included (expandable)
- Support: Priority + SLA response targets


Overage & Add-ons (Suggested)
-----------------------------
- Additional requests (Starter/Growth/Business): metered overage per 100k requests.
- Additional log retention packs: optional monthly add-on.
- Extra admin seats (Growth/Business): per-seat monthly add-on.
- Dedicated onboarding / architecture session: one-time add-on.


Rollout Notes (Important)
-------------------------
1. Launch Free + Starter first to reduce complexity.
2. Enable Growth after Projects feature is stable.
3. Enable Business after you finalize:
   - SLA terms,
   - fair-use boundaries,
   - overage unit pricing,
   - support staffing.
