Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 27, 2026

We collect minimal operational telemetry needed to run KeyGate (request metadata, status metrics, and error logs). We do not expose provider API keys in frontend responses.

Data we process

  • Project metadata (name, slug, status)
  • Subkey metadata (scoped policies and counters)
  • Request logs (model, latency, tokens, status)
  • Health records and backend error logs

Security controls

  • AES-256-GCM encryption at rest for secret material
  • Project-scoped backend guards
  • Rate limiting and quota enforcement

Contact

For deletion/export requests, contact your KeyGate workspace administrator.
